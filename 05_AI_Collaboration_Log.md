## AI Collaboration Log

### Tools Used

- ChatGPT
- Claude Code
- VS Code tools
- https://dillinger.io/
- https://www.plantuml.com/

### Where AI Helped

- Discovery and problem framing
- Product and UX drafting
- Workflow and prioritization design
- Prototype architecture and coding
- DICOM/windowing implementation
- Test generation and debugging
- Documentation and artifact drafting

### Example Prompts or Workflows

- “Help me define an AI-assisted workflow for prioritizing suspected ICH cases in a CT worklist.”
- “Build the prototype workflow with a simulated AI inference layer and real anonymized DICOM images.”
- “Review the prototype end-to-end and identify issues in the workflow, overlay alignment, and demonstration flow.”

### Where AI Was Weak or Wrong

- Initially produced generic recommendations that required refinement.
- Could not reliably determine clinical findings without human review.
- Some generated assumptions about image findings and laterality required verification against the actual DICOM data.

### Human Corrections

- Verified AI-generated product decisions against the project requirements.
- Selected and mapped the real CT datasets to the prototype cases.
- Reviewed ICH visibility and corrected overlay locations.
- Corrected/validated clinical laterality.
- Decided the final workflow, prioritization logic, scope, and demonstration sequence.

### Final Reflection

- AI significantly accelerated drafting, prototyping, coding, testing, and documentation.
- AI did not replace product-manager judgment, clinical reasoning, prioritization decisions, or validation of the actual CT findings.
- The strongest outcome came from combining AI-assisted execution with human review and correction.
